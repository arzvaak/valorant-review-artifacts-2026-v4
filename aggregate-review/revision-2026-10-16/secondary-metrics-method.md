# Secondary descriptive metric inventory

Compute secondary descriptive metrics from every completed additional rating, player-pooling and retuned-removal probability vector. Report row-balanced accuracy, equal-event-weighted ROC-AUC, canonical-orientation calibration intercept/slope, and equal-width ECE with 5, 10 and 20 bins. Rating inventory includes all raw/calibrated online and frozen-six-hour arms, plus frozen-two-hour arms without duplicating online arms. Player inventory includes all seventeen conditions per task; retuned removals include ten reduced conditions and the original full reference on the same eight supported blocks. Reproduce existing later-feedback secondary metrics directly from their saved scored results.

Use the original later scoring metric implementation, including CUDA double-precision LBFGS for the two-parameter reporting-only reliability diagnostic, its constant/one-class guard and convergence/separation checks. Diagnostic coefficients never modify any probability, threshold, selection, calibration or primary result. These are descriptive point estimates, with no new hypothesis family or model selection. Existing complete-event intervals remain the uncertainty evidence where supplied elsewhere; no coefficient interval is implied for these additional points.

Bind executable code, this method, the scoring implementation, completed forecast freezes, probabilities, keys and permitted evaluation labels by SHA-256. Check saved prediction hashes before reporting. Preserve original results and save this inventory in a new directory. No predictor fitting, raw-data access or protected-data access is involved.
