## online-refresh-journal

Online performance refresh versus the earlier residual and feedback-matched K20, broad nonglobal development population: 55,943 maps / 1,049 events and 25,186 series / 1,047 events. Points and intervals are percentage-point accuracy differences, with row and event weighting distinguished by both color and marker. All four comparisons are retained on a common scale. Pointwise 95% complete-event bootstrap intervals condition on saved fits, omit refitting uncertainty and are unadjusted. The six-hour scheduled availability proxy and global-update exclusion remain explicit. These are post-result exploratory accuracy comparisons, not adjusted probability-loss gains or prospective validation.

## horizons-chronology-journal

Schematic of the two target horizons and permitted chronological stages. Map forecasts assume the observed map and lineups/compositions are known before round one; series forecasts use earlier-appearance/usage proxies. Both use the declared scheduled-start lead, which does not verify historical publication timing. The core block-local source release proxy is two hours; online state uses six hours with strictly prior, equal-time frozen feedback and global exclusion. New unreviewed later-cohort rows are score-only. The five fitting/selection/calibration/evaluation stages describe the original protocol; retained later inference performs no new fit, threshold selection or calibration. This diagram is conceptual, not a measured timeline or proof of prospective operational availability.
